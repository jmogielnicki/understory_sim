Tip

Click to look around. W A S D walk, Shift runs, Space jumps.

Press F to fly free: over the village, out across the lake, up to the ridge.

Walk down to the dock and press E at the boat to take it out among the islands.

Press T to let the day run, and watch the homes light up at dusk.

Press H for the settings: the time of day, the clouds, the wind on the lake.

The land is real: Squam Lake, New Hampshire, from the USGS lidar survey. The village is imagined.
